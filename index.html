  • Python pipeline on the MQTT-IoT-IDS2020 dataset comparing Random Forest, Decision Tree and XGBoost for binary and multiclass attack classification.
  • Leakage-safe evaluation: stratified split, SMOTE on training data only, an untouched test partition, and cross-validation.
  • Built on Mosquitto, Node-RED and Flask, and tested live against SSH brute-force, network scanning and MQTT brute-force attacks.

I re-ran the evaluation after finding that resampling before the split inflated earlier scores. The figures above are the corrected held-out results.
